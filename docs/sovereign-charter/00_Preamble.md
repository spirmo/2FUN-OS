# Sovereign Charter
## Preamble
### Tandil Project

---

## Article 0 — Foundational Declaration

This Sovereign Charter constitutes the supreme governing document of the Tandil Project (hereafter referred to as "the Project").

It establishes the foundational principles, structural framework, authority distribution, governance mechanisms, rights, obligations, and systemic boundaries upon which the Project operates.

All subsequent rules, policies, mechanisms, scoring systems, governance decisions, structural modifications, and derivative documents must conform to this Charter.

In the event of conflict between any internal document and this Charter, this Charter shall prevail.

---

## Article 1 — Nature of the Project

1. The Project is a structured digital ecosystem designed upon principles of:
   - distributed participation,
   - structured competition,
   - cooperative alignment,
   - merit-based advancement,
   - systemic continuity.

2. The Project is not an informal community.  
   It is a governed ecosystem operating under defined authority structures and codified internal law.

3. Participation in the Project constitutes explicit acceptance of this Charter and all subsequent governance instruments derived from it.

---

## Article 2 — Sovereignty and Authority

1. The Project operates as a sovereign internal system with autonomous governance logic.
2. Internal authority derives from:
   - this Charter,
   - formally ratified governance mechanisms,
   - recognized structural bodies defined herein.

3. No custom, informal practice, or implied interpretation shall override the explicit wording of this Charter.

4. Silence within this document shall not be interpreted as permission.  
   Only explicitly granted authorities are considered valid.

---

## Article 3 — Structural Intent

The Charter is established to:

1. Eliminate structural ambiguity.
2. Prevent interpretive manipulation.
3. Define authority boundaries.
4. Preserve systemic continuity independent of any individual.
5. Enable scalable evolution without structural collapse.
6. Protect the integrity of governance and participation.

---

## Article 4 — Continuity Principle

1. The Project shall be structured in such a way that its operation does not depend on the presence, dominance, or continued participation of any single individual.
2. Governance authority shall be institutional, not personal.
3. Systemic integrity shall supersede individual influence.

---

## Article 5 — Supremacy Clause

1. This Charter represents the highest internal authority of the Project.
2. Amendments may occur only through procedures defined within this Charter.
3. Any structural body, including founders, councils, or operational entities, remains bound by this document.

---

## Article 6 — Binding Effect

1. All participants, founders, councils, structural units, and future entities formed within the Project are bound by this Charter.
2. Continued participation implies continued acceptance.
3. Withdrawal from participation does not invalidate obligations incurred during active participation.

---

## Closing Declaration

This Charter enters into force upon its formal recognition and registration within the Project documentation structure.

It serves as the foundational layer upon which all subsequent governance architecture shall be constructed.
